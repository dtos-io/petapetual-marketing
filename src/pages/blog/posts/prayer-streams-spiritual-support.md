Across cultures and throughout history, prayers for the deceased have been a cornerstone of honoring those who have passed. From Buddhist chanting temples to Christian vigils to Jewish mourning traditions, there is a universal understanding that continuous spiritual attention benefits both the departed and those who grieve.

But in our modern, busy lives, maintaining constant prayer can be challenging. This is where prayer streams offer a beautiful solution.

## What Are Prayer Streams?

Prayer streams are continuous, ongoing prayers dedicated to memorials around the clock. Inspired by traditions from around the world—monasteries that maintain perpetual prayer, temples with continuous chanting, prayer wheels that spin endlessly—prayer streams bring this devotion into the digital age.

When you subscribe a memorial to a prayer stream, your loved one is included in prayers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## The Tradition of Continuous Prayer

### Buddhist Traditions

Many Buddhist temples maintain continuous chanting of sutras and mantras. The repetition of sacred texts is believed to generate merit that benefits all beings, including the deceased.

### Christian Traditions

Perpetual adoration and continuous prayer chains have been practiced for centuries. Monasteries and convents often maintain round-the-clock prayer schedules.

### Jewish Traditions

Kaddish, the mourner's prayer, is recited daily. Yahrzeit traditions ensure the deceased is remembered on the anniversary of their passing.

### Taoist Traditions

Taoist ceremonies often include continuous chanting and offerings to ensure the peaceful passage of the deceased.

## Why Continuous Prayer Matters

### Spiritual Comfort

For believers, knowing that prayers are continuously being offered for their loved one provides deep spiritual comfort. It's a way of ensuring they are never forgotten in the spiritual realm.

### Active Remembrance

Prayer streams represent an active, ongoing honoring rather than a one-time service. Your loved one receives continuous attention and blessing.

### Connection to Tradition

Many people feel disconnected from the religious traditions of their ancestors. Prayer streams offer a way to maintain these practices without having to perform them yourself.

### Peace of Mind

Life is busy. You can't personally pray 24/7. Prayer streams ensure that even when you're sleeping, working, or occupied with life's demands, your loved one is being remembered and blessed.

## Complementing Your Own Practice

Prayer streams don't replace personal prayer or religious observance. Instead, they complement it. You can still light candles, recite prayers, attend services, and observe traditions in your own way.

Prayer streams simply ensure that even during the times you can't actively pray, someone—somewhere—is offering blessings on behalf of your loved one.
